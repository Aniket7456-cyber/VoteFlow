About VoteFlow

VoteFlow is a modern, secure online election platform designed to make democratic processes accessible, transparent, and effortless for schools, universities, and organisations worldwide.

🎯

Our Mission

Empower every organisation to conduct fair, transparent elections without technical barriers.

🔒

Security First

Every vote is protected with invitation-code authentication and one-vote enforcement.

⚡

Real-Time

Live results dashboard updates instantly the moment a vote is cast.

📤

Excel / PDF Import

Upload voter lists from Excel or PDF — names, phones and codes extracted automatically.
